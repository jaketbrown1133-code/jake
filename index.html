Awnings & Solar Screens

Two decades of Hudson Valley shade, done right.

For more than 20 years — first as Awning FX, now as Undercover Awning — we've designed, fabricated, and installed retractable awnings, stationary awnings, and solar screens throughout the Hudson Valley. That experience shows in the fit, the finish, and how well every project holds up season after season.

Retractable Awnings

Extend for shade on demand, retract flat against the house when you don't need it. Built for decks, patios, and windows.

Stationary Awnings

Fixed-frame coverage for windows, doorways, and walkways — a permanent solution where retractability isn't needed.

Solar & Privacy Screens

Cut glare and heat, block sightlines, and keep outdoor spaces usable through the brightest part of the day.

Zip Screens

Track-guided screens that zip closed for wind-resistant shade and bug protection, opening a porch or patio into a true outdoor room.
